AI Drafts. Humans Decide.

Unified Inbox

WhatsApp, email, web forms — every customer message lands in one inbox. No more juggling three apps.

AI Reply Drafts

Hit "Generate Draft" — AI writes replies from customer history and your knowledge base. Works with OpenAI, DeepSeek, Moonshot, any compatible endpoint. Falls back to local rule templates (clearly labeled) without a key.

Approval Gate (Iron Rule)

AI drafts only — never auto-sends. Every outbound message needs a human "Approve & Send". Keys live in env vars, never reach the frontend.

Customer Profiles

Structured customer records, multi-role contacts, follow-up history.

Sales Pipeline

Deal stage tracking with win/loss reason capture.

Daily Briefing

Auto-summary each morning: new messages, drafts awaiting approval, follow-up reminders.
