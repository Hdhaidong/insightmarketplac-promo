Tech notes

Built on the pi-mono architecture. Runs locally — your data never leaves your network. Current version is rules-engine based (discovery from daily hot-list scans, extraction via URL-pattern matching); an AI-enhanced version (automatic video understanding) is in development.

Dual-route strategy: this product ships on the PI route (pi-mono). DSH route (desktop + web UI) versions are developed separately.
